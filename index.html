Levels 1-10

Trainer visit

Gear and quests

Pick up Mining and a pickaxe in the starter area, then Blacksmithing for Rough Weightstones. Rotation: Seal of Righteousness up, Judgement, Holy Strike. First talent point at 10.

Levels 11-19

Trainer visit

Gear and quests

Start The Tome of Valor in Stormwind early. In Forever, dungeon quest XP and gear are heavily boosted while run XP is reduced: bring every quest, one run per dungeon. Method also names Rhahk'Zor's Hammer and Scepter of the Abandoned as big weapon power spikes. Ruins of Lordaeron is Horde-only, so skip Vilewalkers.

Levels 20-24

Trainer visit

Gear and quests

Consecration burns mana fast: use it on 3+ targets and ease off in the open world.

Levels 25-30

Trainer visit

Gear and quests

Weapon DPS is king while leveling.

Levels 31-40

Trainer visit

Gear and quests

Plate at 40; keep offensive mail if better. Method says Blacksmithing is the only source of mail Spell Power gear.

Levels 41-50

Trainer visit

Gear and quests

Alcaz order: defeat Zarla, keep the Questioner alive until you accept The Man in the Iron Chains, then kill him, return the key, do More Valuable Than Gold.

Levels 51-60

Keep one good two-hander to 60, then reassess.

Supporting info

Stat priority

  • Weapon DPS is king. 1 weapon DPS = 14 AP (about 7 Strength).
  • Then Stamina = Spirit = Strength = Agility; Hit/Crit; Spell Damage over AP.
  • Slower weapons are preferred for Holy Strike / Seal of Command, never over raw DPS.
  • 1 Spell Power = 0.429 damage on Judgement, Holy Strike, Exorcism.
  • Melee hit cap vs bosses is 9%. Consecration, Exorcism and Holy Wrath are spells.

Leveling rotation

  • Blessing of Might + Devotion/Retribution Aura up; Seal of Righteousness, then Seal of Command at 20.
  • Hammer of Justice, Judgement, Consecration on 3+ targets, Exorcism on undead/demons, Holy Strike, Consecration on 1-2 targets.
  • Seal of the Crusader for dungeon bosses and elites.

Alt. talents 10-30 (Mobalytics)

  • 10-14 Deflection (5/5)
  • 15-16 Improved Judgement (2/2)
  • 17 Conviction 1
  • 18-19 Holy Conduit (2/2)
  • 20 Seal of Command
  • 21-22 Pursuit of Justice (2/2)
  • 23-24 Conviction 2-3
  • 25 Sacred Arbiter
  • 26-28 Sanctified Judgement (3/3)
  • 29 Conviction 4
  • 30 Repentance

For group leveling swap Deflection for Benediction.

Professions, race, UI

  • Skip most artisan professions; Mining + Blacksmithing early for Rough Weightstones; Cooking gives 5% XP when Well Fed.
  • Human is best for pure DPS (about 0.4% over Dwarf); Dwarf is much better vs Beasts.
  • Addons: DungeonJournal (/dj), RestedXP (free 1-20), PallyPower Forever. Forever's UI has Damage Meter, Swing Timer and Cooldown Manager (/cdm) in Options.

Enchants and consumables

Verified against Wowhead's Ret enchants and consumables (24 Nov 2026) and LFCarry's Crusader guide (search snippet). Wowhead's advice: you replace gear quickly while leveling, so save expensive enchants for your weapon or gear that lasts.

Where to use Crusader and other weapon enchants

Crusader: 1.0 PPM proc, +100 Strength for 15s plus a 75-125 heal; needs Enchanting 300 and 4x Large Brilliant Shard + 2x Righteous Orb (LFCarry). Wowhead calls it one of the best melee weapon enchants once you can afford it. It's effectively a level 60 enchant, so put it on one weapon only: your endgame two-hander. Enchants are lost when you swap weapons.
